# How Microsoft Copilot Works

If you're asking about **Microsoft Copilot**, the flow is generally like this:

1. **You type a question (prompt)** into Copilot.
2. Copilot sends that prompt to Microsoft's cloud services for processing.
3. Depending on where you're using Copilot:
   - **Copilot for Microsoft 365** can access content you already have permission to see in apps such as Outlook, Teams, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OneDrive, and SharePoint.
   - **Copilot Chat** can use web information (when enabled) and organizational data you have access to.
4. An AI model analyzes your question, retrieves relevant information, and generates a response.
5. The response is returned to you in the app.

## Where Does My Question Go?

Your prompt is typically sent to Microsoft-hosted AI services. For Microsoft 365 commercial customers:

- Prompts, responses, and retrieved organizational data are processed within Microsoft's service boundary.
- Your organization's security permissions are respected. Copilot cannot access files or emails you don't already have permission to view.
- Microsoft states that commercial customer data from Microsoft 365 Copilot is **not used to train foundation AI models**.

## Can Copilot See All My Files?

No. Copilot can only access:

- Files, emails, chats, and documents that **you already have permission to access**.
- Data made available through the Microsoft 365 services connected to your account.

## Can My Manager See My Prompts?

Usually, your manager cannot automatically see your Copilot conversations. However:

- Organizations may have auditing, compliance, retention, or eDiscovery policies in place.
- Administrators may have access to certain records depending on company policies and Microsoft 365 compliance configurations.
